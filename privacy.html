Privacy Policy – Worm Evolution

Developer: Levond · Contact: Studiolevond@gmail.com · Last updated: 6 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Worm Evolution (Android package com.wormevolution.game, “the app”) handles information. We built the app to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts of our own and we run no servers of our own. Optional online features (leaderboard and cloud save) are provided by Google Play Games Services.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and we do not run servers that receive data from the app.

Your game progress (coins, worms, upgrades, Wormdex, settings) is stored on your device. It is not sent to us. If you do not sign in to Google Play Games, it stays only on your device, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your Android settings);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system, app version and language);
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or completed) and diagnostic data.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in the app: cog button on the main screen → Settings → Privacy & ad choices. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

4. Google Play Games Services (leaderboard and cloud save)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, the app uses Google Play Games Services to provide two optional features:

  • Leaderboard: a score based on your best worm, Gods awakened and Omniworm feasts is sent to Google together with your Play Games player name, profile picture and player ID, and is shown to other players on the leaderboard.
  • Cloud save: a copy of your game progress is stored in your Google account (Google Play Games saved games) so you can restore it on another device or after reinstalling. We cannot read it; only your Google account and this app can.

This is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Games Services terms. You can sign out or delete your Play Games data and saved games at any time in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings. The game works fully without signing in.

4b. Other Google Play services

The app may ask Google Play to show the standard in-app review prompt. This is handled entirely by Google Play; the app does not receive your rating or review.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google you can use the tools in your Google account and your device settings (for example, resetting your advertising ID or opting out of ads personalisation). Residents of the EEA/UK have rights under the GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
